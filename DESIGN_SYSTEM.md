## Primary 3D Identity — Ralvie Intelligence Orb

The primary WebGL visual is an audio-reactive AI intelligence orb.

### Concept

The orb represents Ralvie listening, understanding, processing and responding. It must feel intelligent and alive without appearing like a human character or gaming asset.

### Visual Construction

- Organic displaced sphere or icosahedron
- Custom GLSL vertex and fragment shaders
- Translucent outer surface
- Luminous gradient core
- Subtle Fresnel edge illumination
- Two or three spatial voice-wave rings
- Restrained particle intelligence field
- Dark ambient mesh-gradient environment

### States

- Idle: slow breathing and subtle rotation
- Listening: inward surface movement and contracting rings
- Thinking: increased internal movement and orbiting pulses
- Speaking: outward audio-reactive waves
- Connecting: directional energy movement
- Completed: one soft confirmation pulse

### Interaction

- Use subtle pointer influence
- Do not use visible OrbitControls
- Do not allow unrestricted dragging or zooming
- The object should remain compositionally positioned for the hero content

### Performance

- Use only one major homepage canvas
- Cap device pixel ratio at 1.5
- Reduce particles and shader complexity on mobile
- Pause animation when the tab is hidden
- Pause or reduce animation when off-screen
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Provide a static image fallback
- Avoid expensive real-time shadows
- Avoid heavy postprocessing
- Load the scene only after critical page content

### Style Restrictions

Avoid:

- Realistic human avatars
- Robot heads
- Generic metallic blobs
- Excessive neon glow
- Large particle counts
- Fast random movement
- Gaming-style visual effects
- User-controlled orbit camera
- Heavy bloom
